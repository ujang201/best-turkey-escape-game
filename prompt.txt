Build the best turkey escape game you can: a complete, original game that runs in a desktop browser from static files with no network request, played with keyboard and mouse. The player is a turkey escaping becoming Thanksgiving dinner, chased by the farmer and dodging the oven and the carving knife, with a title screen, a run in play with a HUD of score, distance and lives, and an end screen for caught or escaped with a restart. It must be fun and deep — dodging, jumping, hiding and outrunning — with a farmer who chases and corners the turkey, dangers that grow harder from the farmyard to the kitchen, and a reason to run again. Original Thanksgiving art of the turkey, the farmer, the farm and the kitchen, a coherent autumn palette, and controls that answer at once.
